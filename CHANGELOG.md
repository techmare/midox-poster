# Midox Poster: what changed

The newest version is first. Each section is copied into the matching GitHub release.

## 1.0.10: Add a photo to a product that has none · 24 Nov 2026

- **"Take a photo" and "From gallery"** on a product without a photo. The photo goes into Midox, on that product, so Midox fills up with photos as you post. It also goes straight into this post and your gallery, ready for Facebook.
- Needs Midox 3.0.29 or newer; an older Midox says to update it.

## 1.0.9: Photos or not, all products get their turn · 24 Nov 2026

- **Products without a photo aren't kept for last any more:** the picks mix products with and without photos equally, in a new order every day. What got messages and what waited longest still come first.

## 1.0.8: A quicker opening, with a moving arrow · 19 Nov 2026

- **The opening is about a third faster** (about 0.9 s from the splash to the app).
- **The logo's arrow moves:** it slides into the ring, pulls back with it, then shoots off forward as the ring opens onto the app.

## 1.0.7: 10 shows as 10 · 19 Nov 2026

- **Settings › Good day showed "1" when it was set to 10**: the number's box only had room for one digit. Now it fits two.

## 1.0.6: Everything from the review, fixed · 18 Nov 2026

- **Never a blank screen at start:** if Android skips the splash, the screens show anyway after 2.5 s.
- **No "Open Midox" flash at start:** Today says "Getting your products…" while Midox answers.
- **No more cut-off buttons:** long labels shrink to fit, and the long ones are shorter.
- **The alarm rings again** when the next one comes while the last is still on screen.
- **Photos leave the gallery** when you close a post without posting.
- **"Mark sold" is smarter:** a used item is gone for good; a new model comes back to the picks 14 days later, if Midox still has it.
- **"Got messages" shows "Noted ✓"**, counts once a day, and a second tap undoes it.
- **"Next post is ready" goes away** once you post.
- **Rows animate once per visit**, not every time you scroll back.
- **Posted opens on the list that has something.**
- **Streak freezes left this week** show on Today.
- **Today moves to the new day at midnight**, even with the app open.
- **Switches say "on" / "off"** to TalkBack.
- **Typing your words in Settings no longer lags:** saved when you stop typing.
- **Post mode remembers you opened Facebook**, even if Android closed Poster meanwhile.
- **Facebook category per Midox category:** tap Catégorie in post mode and type it as Facebook shows it, once for all lenses, all bags…
- **The right fix when Poster can't read Midox:** install Midox, update it, or reinstall Poster (when Poster was installed first).

## 1.0.5: Faster posts, smarter picks · 18 Nov 2026

- **What to pick in Facebook's dropdowns.** Under the copy buttons, post mode shows the Catégorie and the État (Neuf, D'occasion – bon état…) to choose, so there's nothing left to think about.
- **"Got messages"** on a live post in Posted: one tap when a buyer writes. Products that get messages come back first when they're due for a repost.
- **Morning nudge** (Settings › Nudges): a quiet reminder at a moment you're already up, like arriving at the shop, before the evening ones.
- **Long-press Poster's icon › Post 1 now**: straight into post mode, like the widget.
- **Undo** on the Done screen, for a mistaken "I posted it".
- **Fresh start**: after a missed streak, Today says "Just 1 today and you're back" instead of showing a zero.

## 1.0.4: Up to 10 a day, spaced out · 18 Nov 2026

- **Good day can go up to 10 posts** (Settings › Daily goal). The minimum that keeps your streak stays as you set it.
- **10 minutes between posts**, so Facebook never sees a burst from your account. While you wait, Start posting shows the countdown and you can still open the next post and copy everything; Open Marketplace unlocks when the time is up.
- **"Next post is ready"**: a quiet notification when the 10 minutes are up, until today's goal is reached.
- The Queue shows "3/10" on busy days instead of a row of dots.

## 1.0.3: Back to Poster's own look, everything moves · 18 Nov 2026

- **Poster's first design is back:** warm off-white, near-black, orange, the dark card with today's ring, Space Grotesk and Inter. Dark mode stays, in the same colours.
- **Opening animation:** open Poster from its icon and you fly through its ring: the middle opens into a window onto the app, which settles into place. Android 12 and up.
- **Everything animates:** lists come in one row after another, the ring sweeps in and fills, numbers roll, tabs and screens slide, the bottom bar's marker glides, switches spring, Copy turns into a drawn tick, the orange button slides from Open Marketplace to I posted it, and the Done screen draws its check.
- The widget and the app icon are back in the first design's colours.

## 1.0.2: Widget, soft alarm, dark mode, Midox's look · 18 Nov 2026

- **Home-screen widget.** Today's posts ("1 / 3") with a bar, your streak, and **Post 1 now**, which opens straight into post mode. Once your streak is safe, its button goes quiet.
- **Soft alarm.** The full-screen alarm starts quiet, gets louder over 30 seconds, and buzzes only after 10. Turn it off in Settings › Start soft for the old all-at-once alarm.
- **Dark mode.** Poster follows your phone: warm charcoal at night, cream by day. The widget too.
- **Midox's look.** Same cream page, serif titles and numbers, white cards and one coral button per screen as Midox, with Midox's little touches: buttons that give under your finger, a tick on − / + and tabs, a "done" buzz when you mark a post.
- **Post mode:** the copy rows show "Copied" once you've copied them, and the coral button moves from Open Marketplace to I posted it once Facebook is open.

## 1.0.1: Post every day without thinking · 18 Nov 2026

- **Today** picks what to post (photos first, the longest since posted first) and shows your ring: 1 post keeps your streak, 3 is a great day.
- **Post mode**: one product, nothing else on screen. Its photos go first in your gallery by themselves, the French title, 1 dh price and text are written. Open Marketplace, copy, paste, done.
- **Posted. Streak saved.** After each post, keep going or stop: both are fine.
- **Nags that get louder**: a quiet reminder, a buzzing pop-up, then a full-screen alarm every 30 minutes until you post. Snoozing needs a 3-second hold (3 a day); "I can't today" needs a reason and keeps your streak.
- **Queue**: the next 7 days plan themselves; swap anything out.
- **Posted**: what's live, what's sinking and due for a repost, what's sold.
- **Takes over from Midox**: your products and photos are read from Midox, and the first time, what you posted lately and your Marketplace words.
- Nothing touches Facebook: Poster only opens the new-listing page and copies text, like Midox did.
